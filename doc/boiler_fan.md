Turn on boiler room fan when it gets too warm.

Only test for this once per minute to avoid jerking the fan around.   And also have 1 degree of hysteresis which should further limit any high frequency start/stop action.

Whenever some fault is detected, like unknowm temperatures, default to turning the fan off.

If the temperature timestamp is more than 5 minutes old, turn the fan off.

As a further precaution against pumping cold air into the boiler room, there is mechancial limit switch that will turn the fan off when the boiler room temp goes below about 70F.
